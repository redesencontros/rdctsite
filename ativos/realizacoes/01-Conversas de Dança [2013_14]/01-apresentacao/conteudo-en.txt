Dance Conversations [2013–2014]

DESCRIÇÃO
An artistic and educational event that brought dance into direct dialogue with diverse artistic languages and practices, through the notions of thinking, experimenting, creating, teaching, learning, presenting, and distributing.

The 2014 edition hosted the project "Delegating Authorships", which carried out a series of participatory actions in public spaces and later became the conceptual starting point for the creation of redesencontros (rdct).
